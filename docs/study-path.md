# Study path (4 weeks)

A realistic plan to get ready in a month. It's designed for 1 to 2 hours a day, but you can adjust it to your own pace.

The most important rule: **understand, don't memorize**. If a topic isn't clear, don't move on. Repeat it.

---

## Week 1 — Fundamentals

Goal: master the foundations of the language. Everything else is built on top of this.

**Topics (`01-fundamentals/`):**

- Day 1: `var`, `let`, and `const`.
- Day 2: Data types.
- Day 3: `null` vs `undefined`.
- Day 4: Equality (`==` vs `===`).
- Day 5: Type coercion.
- Days 6-7: Review. Go back to the questions and answer them out loud without looking.

**Weekly goal:** be able to explain the difference between `==` and `===` and why `typeof null` is `"object"` without hesitating.

---

## Week 2 — Scope, hoisting, and functions

Goal: understand how JavaScript handles variable scope and how functions work in depth.

**Topics (`02-scope-hoisting/` and `03-functions/`):**

- Day 1: Scope (global, function, block).
- Day 2: Hoisting.
- Day 3: Temporal Dead Zone (TDZ).
- Day 4: Closures.
- Day 5: Function declaration vs arrow function.
- Day 6: `this` and callbacks.
- Day 7: Pure functions + review.

**Weekly goal:** be able to explain what a closure is with your own example and understand why `this` changes depending on how you call the function.

---

## Week 3 — Arrays, objects, and async

Goal: handle data with ease and understand JavaScript's asynchronous model.

**Topics (`04-arrays-objects/` and `05-async/`):**

- Day 1: `map`, `filter`, `reduce`.
- Day 2: Destructuring, spread, and rest.
- Day 3: Shallow copy vs deep copy + object methods.
- Day 4: Synchronous vs asynchronous.
- Day 5: Promises.
- Day 6: `async/await`.
- Day 7: Event loop and `setTimeout` + review.

**Weekly goal:** be able to transform an array with `map/filter/reduce` without thinking too hard, and explain the order in which asynchronous code runs.

---

## Week 4 — Challenges, output questions, and mock interviews

Goal: apply everything under pressure, like in a real interview.

**Topics (`06-code-challenges/`, `07-output-questions/`, `08-mock-interviews/`):**

- Day 1: Solve every challenge in `06-code-challenges/` without looking at the solution first.
- Day 2: Answer the output questions in `07-output-questions/` without running the code; then verify.
- Day 3: Mock interview `junior.md` with a timer.
- Day 4: Mock interview `mid-level.md`.
- Day 5: Mock interview `frontend-js.md`.
- Day 6: Review your mistakes from the whole week.
- Day 7: Active rest. Explain a concept to someone else (or record yourself explaining it).

**Weekly goal:** feel comfortable answering out loud, writing code without autocomplete, and predicting the output of tricky code.

---

## After the 4 weeks

- Go back to the topics you struggled with most.
- Redo the mock interviews; you should be faster now.
- Practice explaining concepts to another person. If you can teach it, you understood it.
